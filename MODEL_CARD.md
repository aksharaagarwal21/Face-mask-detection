# Model card: face mask classifier

## Model

| | |
|---|---|
| Task | Classify a cropped face as `with_mask`, `without_mask` or `mask_weared_incorrect` |
| Architecture | EfficientNetV2-B0 (ImageNet-pretrained) → global average pooling → Dense(256, swish) → Dense(3, softmax), dropout 0.3 |
| Input | 160×160 RGB, raw 0–255 pixels (scaling is inside the model); square face crop with 15% context per side |
| File | `models/mask_detector.keras` (Keras 3, 24.8 MB); optional TFLite export via `export.py` |
| Calibration | Temperature scaling, T = 0.4014, fitted on the validation split |
| Face detector | YuNet (OpenCV Zoo, 2023), `models/face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx` |

## Intended use

- Aggregate mask-compliance monitoring of a space (counts, compliance rate,
  alerts), with a person reviewing what the system flags.
- Analysis of photos and recorded video for the same purpose.

**Not intended for** identifying people, automated enforcement or penalties
without human review, or use on populations and camera setups that differ
substantially from the training data without first evaluating it there.

## Data

[Kaggle: andrewmvd/face-mask-detection](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andrewmvd/face-mask-detection):
853 photos with face bounding boxes in PASCAL VOC format.

- Faces smaller than 10 px (shorter side) are dropped, leaving 3,603 faces.
- The split is **by photo** (stratified group k-fold, seed 42), so faces from
  one photo never appear in two splits.

| split | with mask | without mask | incorrect | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| train | 1,934 | 432 | 92 | 2,458 |
| val | 474 | 78 | 11 | 563 |
| test | 469 | 98 | 15 | 582 |

Faces are small: the median face is 23 px on its shorter side.

## Training

The head trains for 3 epochs (backbone frozen, LR 1e-3). The whole backbone is
then fine-tuned with BatchNorm frozen: AdamW (weight decay 1e-4), a 1-epoch
warm-up to 3e-4, then cosine decay, and label smoothing 0.1. Batches are drawn
with class sampling ∝ count^0.5. Augmentation: flips, rotation, zoom, shift,
brightness/contrast, low-resolution simulation (12–48 px) and occasional
grayscale. The checkpoint with the best validation macro-F1 is kept. The
saved run early-stopped after 24 epochs (validation accuracy 99.64%, macro-F1
0.981).

## Limitations and risks

- **Rare class.** "Mask worn incorrectly" has 92 training and 15 test faces.
  Its test recall of 13/15 has a 95% interval of roughly 60–98%, so treat it
  as the least reliable class.
- **Small faces.** Most errors come from detection, not classification:
  faces under ~16 px are often missed.
- **Dataset scope.** The photos are mostly street and crowd scenes from one
  public dataset. The data has not been audited for age, gender, skin tone or
  ethnicity balance, and performance per group is **unmeasured**. It may be
  worse for groups, mask types (e.g. patterned or transparent masks), camera
  angles or lighting conditions that are rare in the data.
- **Hard crops.** The 6 test errors (`models/plots/test_errors.jpg`) are
  mostly blurred, dark or partly occluded faces, where the right label is hard
  to judge even by eye.
- **Calibration** was fitted on 563 validation faces and holds on the test
  split (ECE 0.0096). It may drift on data from a different source.

## Privacy

The system does not recognise identities. It can, however, store frames and
face positions: violation logs (`logs/violations.csv`), session reports,
screenshots and heatmaps. Deployers are responsible for notice, consent,
retention and access control for these files under the rules that apply where
it runs. `/api/predict` does not store uploaded images.
